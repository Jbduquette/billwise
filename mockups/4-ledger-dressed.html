The Ledger, dressed

Same Editorial Ledger, with four layers of richness added. Each can be kept or dropped on its own; the desktop shows the Paper edition, the phone the Ink edition.

Category inks

Muted printer's inks, one per category, always next to its name (never colour alone).

The four layers
  1. Paper with depth: a desk under the page, uneven paper edges, raised cards with soft shadows, letterpress headline.
  2. Livelier motion: a red PAID stamp lands when you settle, figures count up, months turn like pages.
  3. Ink & foil: ink-green masthead, gold-foil figures and rule, a coloured mark per category.
  4. Engraved ornaments: a monthly vignette (October's oak), fleuron dividers, a drop cap.
